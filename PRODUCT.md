# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

MkDocs Material, Markdown, and GitHub Pages, as approved in the implementation plan.

## Users

Customer administrators, program managers, reviewers, and coaching recipients using Service Quality Review for Jira Service Management Cloud.

## Product Purpose

Help customers install, understand, and operate recurring evidence-based human service reviews. Success means a reader can find and complete their task, understand the evidence and access boundaries, and get help safely.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The documentation describes program setup, published rubrics, sampling, assessments, optional AI evidence assistance, calibration, results, corrective work, coaching, access, and retention. Human judgment is authoritative. Availability and licensing must be described accurately; the portal does not announce Marketplace availability.

## Brand Commitments

Use the existing Magnascale and Service Quality Review assets and established forest, cream, and lime identity. Keep language clear, practical, and factual.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing customer guidance, accepted implementation behavior, approved brand artwork, and reviewed synthetic product screenshots. Customer data and internal engineering reports must not be published here.

## Product Principles

- Put the customer's task before implementation detail.
- Explain missing evidence, limited coverage, and uncertainty honestly.
- Keep optional AI assistance subordinate to human review.
- Preserve privacy and source-access boundaries in examples and instructions.
