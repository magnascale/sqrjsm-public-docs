# Subscriptions and product limits

Service Quality Review is preparing for a paid Atlassian Marketplace release. The planned offering is one paid edition using Atlassian's eligible host-user billing, rather than charging only for reviewers or reviewed agents.

The listing and live quote are not yet available. When published, check the Marketplace quote for your actual host subscription, eligible user count, billing cycle, taxes, and evaluation terms. The app's billing cycle must match its host. Annual billing is not automatically cheaper at every user count.

## When the licence changes

| State | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Licence permits new work | Authorized users can use the available review workflow, subject to permissions and feature limits. |
| Licence no longer permits new work | Retained information remains readable and completed results exportable to authorized users under access and retention rules. New periods, reviews, AI assistance, and corrective Jira writes pause. |
| Licence restored | Resume using the available actions. Paid work that failed while unlicensed is not replayed automatically. |

If a new-work action becomes read-only, ask an administrator to check the displayed licence state before changing assignments or access.

## Shared AI allowance

All paid host tiers share the same allowance of **up to 1,200 attempts per installation per UTC month**, including retries. A monetary allowance can stop AI earlier, and two attempts per case version apply.

Failed attempts and unresolved reservations can consume allowance. More seats or annual payment do not enlarge the monthly pool. There is no promised rollover, automatic overage charge, or guaranteed successful-generation count. Manual review remains usable when AI is unavailable, subject to licence and permissions.

## Current product boundaries

- Jira Service Management Cloud; one Atlassian site per installation.
- English-language interface and documentation.
- In-app Updates and banners; no email, Slack, or mobile notifications.
- Weekly/monthly scheduling checked approximately hourly.
- Fixed retention, with no customer retention editor or legal-hold guarantee.
- Calibration uses independent human assessments without AI suggestions.
- A program's Jira project and time zone are fixed after creation.
- No public review-volume or response-time guarantee.

For a deployment or volume question, [contact support](../help/support.md) with the intended workflow. A host-seat price is not a guarantee of supported review volume or retained-history size.
