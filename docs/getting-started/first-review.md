# Run your first review

Start with one known request and one reviewer. You will create a program, publish its criteria, invite the reviewer, and submit an evidence-based assessment.

**You need:** program-creation access, a Jira service project, a suitable request, and a reviewer who can see the request and did not handle it.

## 1. Create a program

Open **Apps → Service Quality Review**, then **Create program**. Give the program a clear team name, choose its service project and time zone, and select reviewers and reviewed agents. You become its first manager.

Choose the project and time zone carefully: they are fixed for that program. A new program is needed to change either.

## 2. Publish the starter criteria

Go to **Program settings → Review criteria**. Inspect the five starter criteria, including instructions, evidence guidance, response scales, weights, and which criteria contribute to the score.

Make any changes, choose **Save draft rubric**, then **Preview publication**. Review the preview and confirm publication. Saving a draft and publishing it are separate actions.

## 3. Check people and activate

In **Program settings → People**, confirm the reviewer and reviewed-agent roster, then save changes. A reviewer needs current Jira/JSM access and cannot review their own work.

Return to **Home** and activate the program when its readiness checks pass.

## 4. Invite a reviewer to one ticket

Choose **Review a specific ticket**. Preview the chosen request, confirm the reviewer, and explicitly invite them. The invitation appears in their review workflow; the reviewer accepts it before starting.

!!! note "Standalone reviews and reports"
    This standalone review stays outside period reports. To include manually chosen requests in a period, add them through that period's sample preparation. They remain labelled separately from random selections.

## 5. Assess and submit

The assigned reviewer opens **My reviews**, accepts the invitation, reads the request, and completes the scorecard. Cite evidence for failing judgments or low ratings, and give reasons where the form requires them. **N/A** and **Not enough evidence** have different meanings; neither is a pass or fail.

Choose **Save draft** to pause, or **Submit review** when the assessment is ready. AI evidence assistance is optional.

For detailed steps, see [Complete a review](../guides/reviews.md).

## Build a recurring practice

Once the first assessment is complete, [prepare a review period](../guides/periods.md) to sample requests and track results. Use [calibration](../guides/calibration.md) to clarify criteria before interpreting differences between reviewers or teams.
