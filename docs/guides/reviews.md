# Complete a review

Your job is to make a human assessment supported by the request record. AI is optional; you can complete the review manually.

## Open your assignment

Go to **My reviews** and open the assigned request. Accept an invitation if prompted, and complete any confirmation that you did not handle the ticket.

You need current program membership, an eligible assignment, an open review, and Jira access to the source. If the app blocks access, ask your manager to check those conditions.

## Read the evidence and criteria together

The workspace places the request evidence next to the scorecard. Read each criterion's instructions, rating anchors, and evidence guidance before judging it. A missing or incomplete record does not establish good or poor service.

| Response | Use it when… |
| --- | --- |
| Pass / Fail | The evidence supports an outcome against a binary criterion. |
| 1–5 | The evidence supports the corresponding published rating anchor. |
| N/A | The criterion does not apply to this request. Give the required reason. |
| Not enough evidence | The criterion applies, but the available evidence cannot establish an outcome. Give the required reason. |

**N/A** is excluded from applicable coverage. **Not enough evidence** lowers assessed coverage and is neither a pass nor a fail.

## Cite what supports your judgment

Select the relevant text in the evidence panel and choose **Cite selected text**, or use a passage's **Cite** action. Check that the evidence was added to the intended criterion.

Failing judgments and ratings of 1 or 2 require supporting evidence and a reason. The form also requires reasons for N/A and insufficient evidence. Pass citations are optional. Keep notes relevant and avoid unnecessary personal information.

If a quote no longer matches the source, reload and reassess the available record. Do not replace a missing citation with a claim that the evidence was once visible.

## Save or submit

Choose **Save draft** to pause. Before leaving, check that the save succeeded.

Answer every criterion, review the assessment and any validation messages, then choose **Submit review**. A submitted assessment is read-only. Use supported reopening or correction actions when a change is needed; earlier submitted revisions remain in history.

Optional [AI suggestions](ai.md) can be applied, edited, or dismissed in the draft. They never submit a review for you.

## Keyboard shortcuts

With a scorecard criterion focused, use **P** or **F** for binary judgments, **1–5** for ratings, **N** for N/A, and **I** for insufficient evidence. Use the up and down arrow keys to move between criteria. Shortcuts do not replace required reasons or citations.
