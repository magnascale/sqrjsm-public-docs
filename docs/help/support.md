# Contact support

For product questions, access problems, and unexpected behavior, contact Magnascale through a private support channel.

| Request | Contact |
| --- | --- |
| Product support | [support@magnascale.com](mailto:support@magnascale.com) or the [contact form](https://magnascale.com/contact-us/) |
| Security report | [security@magnascale.com](mailto:security@magnascale.com); support is an accepted fallback |
| Privacy or data request | [privacy@magnascale.com](mailto:privacy@magnascale.com) |

Support is available **Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 Eastern Time**, excluding Ontario statutory holidays, in English. Requests outside those hours enter the next support day.

## Include enough to investigate

- What action you attempted, what you expected, and what happened.
- The approximate time and time zone.
- The exact visible error or failure category, without private content.
- **Support information**, copied by an administrator from **App settings → Diagnostics**.
- A screenshot only after checking it contains no personal or confidential material.

The Support information summary includes app version, licence state, counts, and recent failure categories. It excludes names, account identifiers, and ticket text. We will never ask for your Atlassian password or API tokens.

Do not send ticket bodies, private review notes, AI prompts/responses, credentials, or raw customer logs as an initial report. Public GitHub issues are for documentation feedback and must contain no customer data. Report security issues privately.

## Initial-response objectives

These are goals during support hours, not resolution guarantees, uptime commitments, or a new contractual SLA.

| Severity | Example | First-response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | App unavailable or a critical function unusable for multiple users without a reasonable workaround; suspected data loss or a serious security incident. | 4 business hours |
| High | Major impairment with partial operation or a workaround. | 1 business day |
| Normal | Non-critical defect, configuration problem, or product question. | 2 business days |
| Low | General questions, documentation feedback, cosmetic issues, or feature requests. | 3 business days |

## Security and privacy

Email security reports rather than opening a public issue. Include a safe description and reproduction steps; avoid sending customer content. Privacy requests should identify the requested action and the relevant organization without including credentials.

For uninstall recovery, contact support within 21 days so a request can be raised through Atlassian. Recovery is not guaranteed. See [Data and retention](../administration/data.md).
